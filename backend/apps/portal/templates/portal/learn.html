Security awareness exercise

This was a simulated phishing email

No harm done — it came from your company's security training programme, and nothing you typed was stored as a password. The goal is to practise spotting these safely, before a real one arrives.

What to look for next time

  • The real sender. Check the full address, not just the display name. Lookalike domains swap or add letters.
  • Where links really go. Hover (or long-press) to see the address before you click.
  • Pressure. Urgent deadlines, account-lock threats and unexpected prizes are there to rush you.
  • Unexpected requests. Be wary of asks for passwords, payment changes or gift cards, even from someone you know.
  • Sign in the usual way. Open the site yourself from a bookmark instead of from an email link.

When in doubt, report it — you'll never be in trouble for reporting.
